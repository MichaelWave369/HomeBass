# Infinite Porch Entry — v0.10

HomeBass now begins outside the house.

The front porch is the narrative and architectural boundary between the wider commons and the private / local HomeBass interior.

## v0.10 behavior

- exterior nighttime pixel scene
- front porch and front door
- doorbell interaction
- porch-light interaction
- persistent entry counter
- fixture Porch presence
- explicit node / transport metadata
- direct transition into the walkable house
- ability to return outside from the house shell

## Why the Porch matters

Infinite Porch should not eventually appear as a settings page.

Its concepts can become world objects:

- discovered peers become people on the porch
- invitations become knocks or doorbell events
- node availability becomes porch lights
- shared rooms become destinations
- trusted connections can become known visitors

The world presentation must not erase protocol truth.

## v0.10 trust boundary

Current porch presence is fixture data.

The UI labels the transport as `FIXTURE` to avoid implying that real Infinite Porch discovery or connectivity already exists.

A future adapter may provide:

- peer discovery
- presence state
- invitations
- signed identity claims
- room / service advertisements
- connection health
- trust or capability metadata

## Privacy rule

Being visible on a Porch must eventually be an explicit presence choice.

A connected peer is not automatically entitled to:
- precise location
- real-world identity
- room history
- media activity
- private messages

HomeBass can make networking friendly without making it invasive.
